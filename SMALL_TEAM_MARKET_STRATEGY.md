# How to Win the Small Team Market (< 500 employees)

## The Problem
- 7shifts: $10-15/month for 1-2 managers
- Your pricing: $18/month flat
- **Gap: You're 20% more expensive**

But 20% doesn't matter if value is 5x higher. Here's how to win.

---

## Option 1: Freemium Model (Recommended)

### Structure
```
Free Plan:
  - Up to 100 employees
  - Up to 5 schedules/month
  - Local scheduling only (no AI)
  - Fair Workweek checker included

Pro Plan: $18/month
  - First 500 employees included
  - 25 schedules/month included
  - All AI features
  - Compliance + overages
```

### Why This Wins
✅ **Zero friction**: Small teams try free, no credit card needed
✅ **Lower barrier**: They see it's better before paying
✅ **Land and expand**: Free users become $18/month when they grow
✅ **Competes with 7shifts**: Free tier is better than 7shifts' free tier

### Economics
- Free tier customer costs you ~$13-14/month (fixed share of RDS)
- If 10% convert to paid: ROI breaks even in 10 months
- If 20% convert: Payback in 5 months
- Growth customers stay longer (LTV improves)

### Risk
- Support cost for free users
- Some never convert
- But: **discovery cost is zero** (they find you organically)

## Option 2: Freemium + Upgrade Incentive

### Structure
```
Free Plan:
  - Up to 50 employees
  - Up to 2 schedules/month
  - Local scheduling only

Pro Plan: $18/month
  - First 500 employees
  - 25 schedules/month
  - AI scheduling
  - Fair Workweek enforcement
```

### Upgrade Triggers (Built-in Nudges)
- Hit 50 employees → "Upgrade to unlock more"
- Hit 2 schedules → "Upgrade for unlimited"
- Click "AI Schedule" button → "Upgrade to use AI"

### Why This Works
✅ **Natural upgrade path**: They hit limits when they need to
✅ **Justifies price**: "You've grown past free tier"
✅ **Win on value**: By the time they upgrade, they've seen AI work

### Realistic Conversion
- Free → Pro conversion: 15-25% (industry standard for SaaS)
- If 20 customers sign up free: 3-5 convert to Pro
- At $18/month, that's $54-90/month revenue from 20 free signups

## Option 3: Price Tiering by Market Segment

```
Starter Plan: $12/month
  - Up to 300 employees
  - 15 schedules/month
  - Local scheduling only
  - No AI, no compliance

Pro Plan: $18/month (your current base)
  - Up to 500 employees
  - 25 schedules/month
  - AI scheduling included
  - Fair Workweek compliance
```

### For Compliance-Focused (Healthcare, Fast-Food)
```
Compliance Plan: $25/month
  - Everything in Pro
  - Fair Workweek enforcement
  - Overtime monitoring
  - Regulatory reports
```

### Why This Works
✅ **Price for value**: Compliance customers pay more, have higher LTV
✅ **Win on price**: Starter plan ($12) beats 7shifts ($10-15)
✅ **Segment the market**: Don't try to be everything to everyone

## Option 4: "Loss Leader" Small Team Plan

### Structure
```
Starter: $9/month for first 6 months, then $18
  - Limited to 100 employees
  - Limited to 10 schedules/month
  - Local scheduling only

Pro: $18/month (as planned)
```

### Why This Works
✅ **Beat 7shifts on price** ($9 vs $10+)
✅ **Acquire aggressively** at low price
✅ **Convert to Pro** when they grow (usually in 3-6 months)
✅ **Recover CAC** when they upgrade

### Economics
- Give: $9/month × 6 months = $54 acquisition cost
- Get back: $18/month × 24 months (LTV) = $432
- **3-month payback**, then pure profit

### Risk
- Need to forecast conversion rate accurately
- Support costs for small teams

## Option 5: Free Trial (Lower Friction)

### Structure
```
Free 14-day trial of Pro Plan
  - Full access to AI, compliance, unlimited
  - No credit card required
  - Auto-converts to $18/month if not canceled

OR

Free 30-day trial
  - Full Pro access
  - Credit card required
  - Converts at ~30-50% (higher commitment)
```

### Why This Works
✅ **No price objection**: They try full product free
✅ **See the value**: AI scheduling "wow moment" comes fast
✅ **Industry standard**: Most SaaS uses this

### Conversion Rates
- 14-day trial: 15-25% conversion (lower commitment)
- 30-day trial: 30-50% conversion (higher quality)

## Option 6: Vertical-Specific Play (Fast-Food/Healthcare)

### For Fast-Food Restaurants
```
Fair Workweek Plan: $18/month
  - Includes Fair Workweek compliance checking
  - Free schedule audits
  - Targeted at "need compliance + affordability"

Positioning: "Fair Workweek compliance for $18 vs hiring a consultant for $1000+"
```

### Why This Works
✅ **Solves real pain**: NYC fair workweek violations cost $500-1000 per incident
✅ **Value = ROI**: $18/month saves $1,000+ risk
✅ **Narrow market**: Easier to win than "all small teams"
✅ **Word-of-mouth**: Fast food networks talk to each other

### Target: Single-location restaurants and small chains
- Often don't know about Fair Workweek law
- Can't afford consultants
- $18/month is a no-brainer

```
Month 1:
  Free tier cost:        $260 (20 × $13 fixed cost)

  Free tier cost:        $520 (40 × $13)

Month 12 (if 20% convert, 10 months in):
  Free tier cost:        $780 (60 × $13)
  Converted customers:   12 × $18 = $216 + 6 × $18 (early converts still there) = $324

Month 24:
  Free tier cost:        $1,040 (80 × $13)
  Converted:             $720/month from conversions
  Net:                   -$320 (break-even near)

```

**By month 18-24, freemium becomes cash-flow positive.**

## Bottom Line

**You can't win on price with a $18 flat fee competing against $10 managers.**

**But you can win on:**
1. **Freemium** (zero friction entry)
2. **Value** (AI, compliance, unlimited)
3. **Verticals** (Fast-food compliance as value driver)

**Recommended move: Freemium + vertical pricing**
- Freemium acquires volume from small teams
- Vertical plans (compliance, healthcare) monetize fast-growing segments
- Converts naturally as customers grow
- Word-of-mouth drives small-team adoption (restaurants tell each other about compliance)
