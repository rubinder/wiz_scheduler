PRIVACY POLICY FOR WIZSCHEDULER

Effective Date: April 5, 2026
Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
WizScheduler ("we", "our", "us") is a multi-tenant AI-powered employee scheduling platform operated by Suggestival LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal data when you use our service. We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the New York SHIELD Act, and other applicable data protection laws.

2. DATA WE COLLECT
We collect the following categories of personal data:

A. ACCOUNT INFORMATION
- Full name, email address, hashed password
- Company name and location information
- Phone number (optional)
- Account preferences and language settings

B. EMPLOYEE PROFILE DATA
- Full name, email address (if provided), phone number (optional)
- Assigned roles and job titles
- Skill levels and competency ratings
- Work location assignments
- Hire date and employment status
- Seniority ratings

C. AVAILABILITY AND SCHEDULING DATA
- Weekly availability windows (day, start time, end time)
- Shift preferences and constraints
- Vacation and time-off requests
- Shift history and assigned shifts
- Schedule compliance flags (Fair Workweek violations, etc.)

D. WORK PREFERENCE DATA
- Interpersonal affinity scores between employees (used to optimize team composition and reduce conflicts)
- Affinity expiration dates
- Team preference annotations
- Pay rate information (for scheduling signals)
- Overtime and seniority signals

E. TECHNICAL DATA
- IP addresses (recorded during login and API requests)
- JWT authentication tokens (not stored server-side beyond session)
- Browser type and version
- Device identifiers
- Timestamps of access

F. USAGE DATA
- API request logs for error monitoring
- Feature usage patterns
- Schedule generation history
- Check-in activity (location, time, date)

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
We process your personal data for the following purposes:

A. SCHEDULE GENERATION (PRIMARY USE CASE)
Your availability, roles, skill levels, seniority, pay rates, affinities, and preferences are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to generate optimized weekly shift schedules. Data is transmitted via encrypted API calls through Anthropic's commercial API. Anthropic does not use it to train AI models and keeps it only for the limited period its commercial terms allow (for example for trust and safety review).

B. ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
To create and maintain your user account, authenticate sessions via JWT, manage multi-tenant company access, and enforce role-based access controls.

C. COMMUNICATION
To send transactional emails (welcome, password resets, schedule notifications, schedule generation results) via our email service provider, Resend. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit opt-in.

D. DATA IMPORT/EXPORT
To facilitate integration with 7shifts, Deputy, Homebase, and other scheduling systems for importing and exporting employee and schedule data at your request.

E. COMPLIANCE CHECKING
To validate schedules against Fair Workweek and other labor law requirements, and to generate compliance reports.

F. SERVICE IMPROVEMENT
To monitor errors, analyze usage patterns, improve scheduling algorithms, and develop new features based on aggregate, anonymized data.

G. SECURITY AND FRAUD PREVENTION
To detect and prevent unauthorized access, monitor for abuse, and maintain service security.

4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (GDPR)
We process your data based on:
- Consent: You provide explicit consent during registration for data processing and AI-powered scheduling.
- Contractual Necessity: Processing is necessary to provide the scheduling service you have requested.
- Legitimate Interest: For security, fraud prevention, service improvement, and compliance with law.
- Legal Obligation: To comply with labor law reporting requirements and court orders.

5. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY PROCESSORS
We share your data with the following third-party processors only when necessary:

A. ANTHROPIC (CLAUDE AI)
- Purpose: AI-powered schedule generation
- Data Shared: Employee names, roles, skill levels, availability, affinities, preferences
- Safeguards: Data transmitted via encrypted API calls through Anthropic's commercial API; not used for model training; kept by Anthropic only for the limited period its commercial terms allow
- Legal Basis: Your consent and contractual necessity

B. THIRD-PARTY SCHEDULING PLATFORMS
- Purpose: Employee and schedule data import/export
- Data Shared: Employee profiles, shift schedules, and related workforce data
- Examples: 7shifts, Deputy, Homebase
- Safeguards: Data transferred only at customer's explicit request; encrypted in transit; user controls which fields are exported
- Legal Basis: Your explicit instruction

C. RESEND (EMAIL SERVICE PROVIDER)
- Purpose: Transactional email delivery
- Data Shared: Email addresses, recipient names, email content
- Safeguards: Data processed solely for email delivery; encrypted in transit; Resend does not use data for marketing
- Legal Basis: Contractual necessity

D. CLOUD INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER
- Purpose: Data storage and hosting
- Data Shared: All application data including user accounts, employee profiles, availability, schedules
- Safeguards: Encryption at rest and in transit; access restricted to application service; regular backups
- Legal Basis: Contractual necessity

E. AMAZON WEB SERVICES (AI HOST)
- Purpose: Phone calls to a restaurant's AI host (Amazon Connect, Amazon Lex, Amazon Polly, AWS Lambda) and the AI host analytics store (Amazon S3, AWS Glue, Amazon Athena), in the United States (region us-east-1)

- Safeguards: Calls are not recorded; Contact Lens and Lex conversation logs are not turned on; analytics records are deleted after 365 days and can be queried only through a role that requires multi-factor authentication
- Legal Basis: The restaurant's instructions, as its processor (see Section 17)

F. STRIPE (PAYMENTS)
- Purpose: Subscription billing and card payments, including the AI host subscription
- Data Shared: Customer billing contact details, payment card details, invoices, and AI host usage amounts per billing period; no diner data
- Safeguards: Card details are collected by Stripe Checkout and not stored by WizScheduler; encrypted in transit
- Legal Basis: Contractual necessity

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

6. DATA RETENTION
We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services:
- Active Account: All data retained during the service period
- After Account Deletion: All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days, including employee profiles, availability, affinities, shift history, check-ins, and consent records
- Pay Records: Time and attendance entries used for payroll are kept for 7 years from their pay date on your employer's instruction, including after an individual account is deleted (the pay record keeps the employee's name), and are then deleted automatically
- After Subscription Cancellation: The employer's account is read-only for 90 days, then deleted together with all of its data, including pay records. If the employer bought the optional payroll archive, one copy of its pay records (employee names, locations, roles, shift times, paid hours and check-in times) is kept in encrypted storage for 7 years after the account is deleted and then deleted automatically. Only the employer's administrators at the time the account is deleted can retrieve it. If the employer did not buy the archive, keeping any pay records it is required to keep after deletion is the employer's responsibility, not ours
- Compliance Records: We may retain limited audit logs for up to 1 year to comply with legal obligations
- Backups: Data in database backups is encrypted and retained per our backup policy (typically 30 days)

8. DATA SECURITY

- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plaintext
- Authentication via signed JWT tokens with configurable expiration
- Multi-tenant data isolation ensures company data is strictly separated at the application and database level
- All API communications are encrypted via TLS 1.2 or higher
- Security headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options) are enforced
- Regular security updates and vulnerability monitoring
- Data encrypted at rest in database backups
- Limited access control: only authorized service components access user data

While we implement robust security measures, no system is 100% secure. We maintain cyber insurance and breach notification procedures.

17. DINERS OF RESTAURANTS THAT USE THE AI HOST
Some restaurants that use WizScheduler also use its AI host, which takes reservations from their diners by web chat and by phone. This section applies to you if you book, change, cancel or ask about a reservation with such a restaurant, or if the restaurant records your reservation in WizScheduler. Diners are not WizScheduler users.

A. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE FOR YOUR DATA
The restaurant is the controller of its diners' personal data. WizScheduler processes that data on the restaurant's behalf, as its processor, under our Data Processing Agreement with the restaurant. Please send questions and requests about your data to the restaurant.

B. DATA WE PROCESS FOR THE RESTAURANT
- Your name and phone number, given for the reservation
- Your email address, if the restaurant records one (the AI host does not ask for it)
- Party size, the date and times of the reservation, notes on it, and its status and confirmation code
- Transcripts of your conversations with the AI host: what you write in the web chat or say on the phone (as recognized from speech), and the AI host's replies
- On phone calls, your caller ID number
- On web chat, a keyed hash of your IP address, used for rate limits and to stop repeated guessing of booking details. Raw IP addresses are not stored

C. HOW THE DATA IS USED
- To make, change, cancel and look up reservations, and to pass you to the restaurant's staff when the AI host cannot help
- To screen phone calls before the AI host answers. Your caller ID number is checked against the restaurant's own allow and block lists and against the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's published Do Not Call reported-calls data. A number is treated as spam and the call is ended when that data shows at least 3 reports of it in the last 90 days, unless the restaurant has allowed the number. Calls with a withheld caller ID are answered. The record of each screening decision keeps only a keyed hash of the caller number, not the number itself
- To bill the restaurant for its use of the AI host. Usage records hold no diner data

D. AI PROCESSING
The text of your conversation and the reservation details the AI host looks up are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to produce the AI host's replies (see Section 5.A).

E. PHONE CALLS
Phone calls to the AI host are handled by Amazon Web Services in the United States (region us-east-1): Amazon Connect carries the call, Amazon Lex turns your speech into text, Amazon Polly speaks the AI host's replies, and AWS Lambda passes each turn of the conversation to WizScheduler.
- Calls are not recorded
- Amazon Connect Contact Lens (call analytics) is not turned on, and Amazon Lex conversation logs are not turned on
- Amazon Connect's call flow logging is switched off at the start of every call, so it does not record your caller ID number, and any flow logs are deleted after 1 day
- Amazon Connect keeps its own record of each call, including the caller ID number, for the period AWS sets for Amazon Connect contact records (currently 24 months)
- What you say is turned into text by Amazon Lex, and the AI host's replies are spoken by Amazon Polly, under AWS's service terms, which allow AWS to use that content to improve its AI services
- The logs of our AWS Lambda functions record a call identifier and the outcome, not your number or what you said, and are deleted after 14 days

F. RETENTION
- Conversation transcripts are deleted 1 year after the conversation started
- Your name, phone number, email address and notes are removed from a reservation 1 year after the reservation's end time. The reservation itself (party size and times, without your contact details) is kept for the restaurant's statistics
- Records of call screening decisions are deleted after 1 year
- Entries for your number on a restaurant's allow or block list are deleted 1 year after they expire; entries without an expiry stay until the restaurant removes them
- Records of failed booking lookups, used to stop repeated guessing, are deleted after 1 day
- If the restaurant's WizScheduler account is deleted, its diner data is deleted with it

G. ACCESS AND ERASURE
To see or erase your data, contact the restaurant. The restaurant can export your data at one of its locations, and erase it, using your phone number; it can also erase a single reservation and its conversations using the reservation's confirmation code. Erasure deletes your conversation transcripts and that location's allow or block list entries for your number, and removes your name, phone number, email address and notes from your reservations there. Entries that apply to all of a restaurant group's locations are removed separately by the group's administrator. Conversations are found by the caller ID number they came from, or by their link to a reservation made with your phone number. A web chat that did not lead to a reservation, and a call made with a withheld number, cannot be found by phone number. A conversation that is in progress at the moment of erasure is closed rather than deleted; it is deleted when the restaurant runs the erasure again.

H. ANALYTICS
We measure how the AI host performs (for example, how many conversations end in a reservation) in a separate analytics store with Amazon Web Services. It holds one record per conversation, reservation, call screening decision and usage charge, with fields such as channel, outcome, party size, times, turn counts, costs and response times. It holds no diner names, phone numbers, email addresses or confirmation codes, no text written by the diner or the AI host (only the length of each message), and no other key that identifies a diner. Records in it are deleted after 365 days. Only a role that requires multi-factor authentication can query it. The records carry internal identifiers that link to the restaurant's data in WizScheduler until that data is deleted. We use them, and totals derived from them, to run, measure, secure and improve WizScheduler. Daily totals derived from it are shown to the restaurant in WizScheduler.

We do not sell diner data.
