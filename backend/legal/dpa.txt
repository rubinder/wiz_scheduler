DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT FOR WIZSCHEDULER

Effective Date: April 5, 2026
Last Updated: October 31, 2026

1. SCOPE AND PURPOSE
This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between Suggestival LLC, operating as WizScheduler ("Processor") and the customer organization ("Controller"). It governs the processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in connection with the WizScheduler scheduling service, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), CCPA, and other applicable data protection laws.

2. DEFINITIONS
Terms used in this DPA have the meanings given in the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) unless otherwise defined herein:
- "Personal Data": Any data relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed through the Service.
- "Processing": Any operation performed on data (storage, retrieval, transmission, optimization, etc.).
- "Data Subject": An employee or person whose data is processed (not the organization).
- "Controller": The organization that owns the WizScheduler account and determines how data is used.
- "Processor": Suggestival LLC, which processes data on the Controller's behalf.

3. PROCESSING DETAILS

A. SCOPE
- Subject Matter: Provision of AI-powered employee scheduling services.
- Duration: For the term of the service agreement, plus as required for legal compliance.
- Nature and Purpose: Storage, retrieval, transmission to AI models, and optimization of employee scheduling data.

B. TYPES OF PERSONAL DATA
- Employee names, email addresses, phone numbers
- Work availability windows, shift preferences, constraints
- Role assignments, skill levels, seniority ratings
- Interpersonal affinity scores and team preferences
- Shift history, schedule assignments
- Pay rate information, overtime signals
- Check-in activity (location, date, time)
- Technical data (IP addresses, device identifiers)

C. CATEGORIES OF DATA SUBJECTS
- Employees of the Controller's organization
- Managers and administrators

D. DURATION
- During active subscription: Data is retained and processed.
- After termination: When the subscription is cancelled, the account becomes read-only for 90 days, during which the Controller can reactivate it or export its data. At the end of those 90 days all Personal Data is deleted, except as set out in Section 12B.

4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PROCESSOR

A. PROCESSING INSTRUCTIONS
The Processor shall:
- Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller
- Process data solely for the purposes specified in these Terms and Privacy Policy
- Not process data for any other purpose without explicit Controller instruction
- Comply with the Controller's data handling preferences and restrictions

B. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Processor shall:
- Ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data have committed to confidentiality
- Restrict access to Personal Data to employees who need it to perform their duties
- Provide annual confidentiality training to relevant staff
- Require third-party employees (contractors, consultants) to sign confidentiality agreements

C. TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL SECURITY
The Processor shall implement and maintain:
- Encryption of data at rest (AES-256 or equivalent) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Secure password hashing using bcrypt
- JWT-based authentication with configurable token expiration
- Multi-tenant data isolation at the application and database level
- Security headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, CSP)
- Firewalls and intrusion detection systems
- Regular security updates and patches
- Vulnerability scanning and penetration testing (annually)
- Access logs and audit trails

D. ASSISTANCE WITH RIGHTS REQUESTS
The Processor shall:
- Assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests within 30 days
- Provide extracted data in a machine-readable format (JSON, CSV) upon request
- Delete or return Personal Data upon instruction
- Certify deletion in writing
- Not charge additional fees for reasonable assistance requests

E. DELETION OR RETURN
The Processor shall, at the Controller's choice:
- Permanently delete all Personal Data upon termination of the service
- Return all Personal Data to the Controller in a structured format
- Certify deletion or return in writing
- Delete data from all backups within 30 days (encrypted backups may be retained longer for recovery purposes)

F. COMPLIANCE DOCUMENTATION
The Processor shall:
- Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA
- Maintain records of processing activities (Purpose, Retention, Recipients)
- Provide compliance certifications upon request
- Document security measures and incident response procedures

G. BREACH NOTIFICATION
The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of a confirmed or suspected Personal Data breach, providing:
- The nature of the breach and categories of data affected
- Approximate number of data subjects affected
- Likely consequences of the breach
- Measures taken or proposed to mitigate the breach
- Contact point for further information

5. SUB-PROCESSORS

A. AUTHORIZED SUB-PROCESSORS
The Controller authorizes the use of the following sub-processors listed in the processors table of this document:
- Anthropic (AI schedule generation)
- 7shifts, Deputy, Homebase (data export/import)
- Resend (email delivery)
- Cloud infrastructure provider (data hosting)

B. SUB-PROCESSOR CHANGES
The Processor shall:
- Notify the Controller at least 30 days in advance of any intended changes to sub-processors
- Provide information about the new sub-processor's security practices
- Give the Controller the opportunity to object to the new sub-processor
- Allow the Controller to delete their account if they object to a sub-processor change
- Ensure that sub-processors are bound by data protection obligations equivalent to this DPA
- Remain liable to the Controller for sub-processor performance

6. DATA TRANSFERS

A. INTRA-EU TRANSFERS
Data transfers within the EU/EEA comply with GDPR without additional safeguards.

B. TRANSFERS OUTSIDE THE EU/EEA
Where Personal Data is transferred outside the EU/EEA (e.g., to the US for Anthropic API calls or cloud hosting), the Processor ensures appropriate safeguards are in place:
- EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are executed with each sub-processor
- Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs) are conducted per GDPR Article 46
- Supplementary measures are implemented as necessary to ensure adequate protection
- The Controller is notified of any legal challenges to adequacy decisions

C. RECIPIENT COUNTRIES
Personal Data may be transferred to:
- United States (Anthropic, Resend, cloud infrastructure)
- Canada (7shifts)
- Other jurisdictions as required for service delivery

7. SECURITY MEASURES

A. TECHNICAL MEASURES
- Encryption of data at rest in the primary database (AES-256)
- Encryption of data in transit via TLS 1.2+ (minimum cipher strength: 128-bit)
- Secure password hashing using bcrypt (cost factor ≥ 10)
- JWT authentication with signed tokens (HS256 or RS256)
- Configurable token expiration (default 1 hour)
- Secure session management

B. ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES
- Multi-tenant data isolation enforced at the application and database level
- Role-based access control (RBAC) restricting access by job function
- Principle of least privilege: employees access only data they need
- Regular security awareness training for all staff
- Background checks for employees with data access
- Confidentiality agreements signed by all employees
- Non-disclosure agreements with contractors

C. MONITORING AND LOGGING
- API request logging (not including request bodies)
- Access logs for database connections
- Audit trails for configuration changes
- Intrusion detection system (IDS) monitoring
- Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) for alert correlation
- Log retention: 30 days for access logs, 1 year for audit logs

D. INCIDENT RESPONSE
- Documented incident response procedures
- 24/7 on-call incident response team
- Rapid containment and remediation procedures
- Regular incident response drills

8. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

A. TIMING
The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of a confirmed or suspected Personal Data breach.

B. NOTIFICATION CONTENT
Notification shall include:
- The nature of the breach (unauthorized access, accidental deletion, encryption key compromise, etc.)
- Categories of data affected (employee names, availability data, etc.)
- Approximate number of data subjects affected
- Probable consequences of the breach
- Measures taken or proposed to mitigate the breach and prevent recurrence
- Contact point for the data protection officer or other responsible person

C. INVESTIGATION
The Processor shall conduct a prompt investigation to determine the scope and impact of the breach.

D. CONTROLLER RESPONSIBILITY
The Controller remains responsible for notifying data subjects and regulatory authorities where required under GDPR Article 33-34.

9. AUDITS AND INSPECTIONS

A. RIGHT TO AUDIT
The Controller or an authorized independent auditor may conduct audits of the Processor's compliance with this DPA.

B. NOTICE
The Controller shall provide reasonable notice (at least 15 days) before conducting an audit, except in the case of security incidents.

C. COOPERATION
The Processor shall:
- Provide access to relevant facilities, equipment, and records
- Respond to auditor questions and requests
- Provide certifications of compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Not charge additional fees for reasonable audit requests (more than one audit per year may incur reasonable costs)

D. REMEDIATION
The Controller or auditor may recommend corrective actions. The Processor shall address findings within 30 days of the audit report.

10. STANDARD CONTRACTUAL CLAUSES (SCCs)
For transfers of Personal Data outside the EU/EEA, this DPA incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module One: Controller-Processor). The Processor ensures that sub-processors also execute SCCs or equivalent safeguards.

11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Nothing in this DPA limits the liability of either party for:
- Personal Data breaches or violations of data protection laws
- Breach of confidentiality obligations
- Fraud or gross negligence

Liability for all other claims remains subject to the limitation of liability in the Terms of Service.

12. TERMINATION

A. UPON EXPIRATION OR TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT
Within 90 days of termination (the read-only period that follows cancellation), the Processor shall, at the Controller's written choice:
- Permanently delete all Personal Data
- Return all Personal Data in a structured, machine-readable format
- Certify deletion or return in writing

B. DATA RETENTION FOR LEGAL COMPLIANCE
The Processor may retain limited Personal Data for as long as required by applicable law (e.g., for tax compliance, audit trails, legal holds).

Pay records (the time and attendance entries used for payroll) are retained on the Controller's instruction for seven (7) years from their pay date, including after an individual's account is deleted, and are then deleted automatically. The Controller can export them at any time, including during the 90-day period after cancellation. When the Controller's account is deleted at the end of that period, its pay records are deleted with it, unless the Controller has bought the optional payroll archive described in Section 12D.

C. BACKUP RETENTION
Encrypted database backups may be retained for an additional 30 days for disaster recovery purposes, after which they must be securely destroyed.

D. OPTIONAL PAYROLL ARCHIVE
During the 90-day period after cancellation, the Controller may instruct the Processor to keep a copy of its pay records after its account is deleted, for the one-time fee set out in the Terms of Service. On that instruction:
- When the account is deleted, the Processor writes one file containing the Controller's complete pay-record history (employee names, locations, roles, shift times, paid hours and check-in times) to private, encrypted storage with its hosting provider (Amazon Web Services, United States)
- The file is kept for seven (7) years from the date it is written and is then deleted automatically. Because it is written at deletion, some records in it may be kept longer than seven years from their pay date; the Controller instructs this retention
- The file is used for no other purpose and is released only to the email addresses of the Controller's administrators recorded when the archive was bought, through a single-use link sent to that address
The archive is optional. Exporting pay records and having them deleted remains available at no charge; the fee is for storage after the service ends, not for returning the Controller's data.
